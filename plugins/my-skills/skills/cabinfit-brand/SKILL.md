# CabinFit brand

CabinFit sells aviation-themed jackets, vests, tracksuits and accessories from Portugal.
The store is a Shopify theme based on Dawn (Liquid).

## Look

- Style: simple and classic. Chosen direction: "refined classic", copper and charcoal on white.
- Page background is white (client requirement). Never a dark page, even though the logo file sits on black.
- Text uses the shape and colors of the logo, or close to them.
- Palette: white or off-white ground, charcoal text, copper as the only accent, the airplane's grey as a secondary neutral.

## Logo

- Copper/terracotta serif lettering "CABINFIT", a "CF" monogram with a dashed arc, and a grey airplane.
- Source files are PDF or Canva, not vector. Ask for the files rather than redrawing the monogram or plane.

## Rules

- Bright copper on white fails contrast for small text. Use a darker copper (at least 4.5:1 on the background) for body text, labels and links; keep the bright copper for large display type and rules.
- Display type: a serif that echoes the logo. Pair it with a quiet sans or mono for small labels.
- Aviation references stay subtle (codes such as LIS or OPO, item numbers, ruled lines, a dashed arc). No cartoon planes, propellers, clouds or sky-blue gradients.
- Product photos are never filtered: customers buy by color.

## Store facts that affect design

- Languages: PT, EN, ES, FR. Currencies: EUR and GBP (USD to be confirmed).
- Out-of-stock variants show "coming soon" or a "notify me when back in stock" option.
- Client-facing copy is in European Portuguese (pt-PT).
